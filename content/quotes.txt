# Quotes for the terminal greeting. One per line. Blank lines and # comments ignored.
#
# ORIGINAL WORK. These are written for this repo in the register of a gothic
# amusement dark ride — they are not a transcription of any ride's script. Keep it
# that way: if you can hear a specific recorded voice saying it verbatim, rewrite it.
#
# Style: dry, courteous, faintly threatening. The host is delighted you came and
# has no intention of letting you leave. Keep each under ~90 characters so it sits
# on one line in a narrow terminal.

Do not be alarmed. The walls are supposed to do that.
There is no turning back now. There is, however, a gift shop.
Kindly keep your hands, arms and soul inside the buggy at all times.
The management assumes no responsibility for anything that follows you home.
Our ghosts are perfectly harmless. Our paperwork is another matter.
Nine hundred and ninety-nine haunts reside here. Room, as ever, for one more.
Mind the step. It has a habit of not being there.
The candles were lit some time ago. Nobody remembers by whom.
We keep the lights low. The residents prefer to be suggested rather than seen.
Every door in this corridor is locked. Every door in this corridor is knocking.
The portrait is watching you. It considers this its job.
The organ plays nightly. The organist declined to stay for the applause.
Your reservation was made a very long time ago.
The clock has no hands. It is always precisely late enough.
Something is in the seat behind you. It is being very polite about it.
The wallpaper is not moving. The wallpaper would like you to stop staring.
Do sign the guest book. Most visitors find they have already signed it.
The staircase leads nowhere, which has never discouraged anyone from climbing it.
A cold draught means nothing. It means nothing at all. Please keep walking.
We have no exits. We have only doors that are used in the other direction.
The mirror adds one to every reflection. Do not count.
Dust settles here in the shape of footprints.
The house does not creak. The house is clearing its throat.
Your host is dead, but remains extremely attentive.
Please do not feed the spectres. They are on a very strict diet of visitors.
The raven has been here longer than the house. Nobody has told it that.
Tea is served at midnight. Attendance is not optional.
The crystal ball is only a formality. She already knows.
There is a hitchhiker in your terminal. It seemed rude to mention it earlier.
The graveyard is a lively place, all things considered.
Every mirror in this house is honest. That is the problem with them.
The chandelier has not fallen yet. Yet is doing a great deal of work in that sentence.
We are so glad you could make it. We were fairly certain you would.
Your coat has been taken. Your coat will not be returned.
The east wing is closed. The east wing has always been closed. There is no east wing.
Listen: that is the sound of the house counting.
A séance is in progress. You are, regrettably, the subject.
The floorboards remember everyone. The floorboards are not discreet.
Do enjoy your stay. Do is such a hopeful little word.
The bride has been waiting in the attic. She is very patient and very married.
Nothing here is haunted. Everything here is simply still occupied.
The exit sign is a decoration. We felt the room needed something cheerful.
